# Meet Minds Studio: Build an AI Team That Actually Gets Work Done

Imagine opening one workspace and seeing your own team of AI collaborators.

One researches. One writes. Another helps organize a project. A fourth monitors useful information and prepares a daily briefing.

You can talk to them, give them the right project context, assign work and see when the results are ready for review.

That is **Minds Studio**.

Minds Studio is a workspace where you collaborate with AI assistants called **Minds**. Instead of beginning with an empty chat every time, you can give each Mind a clear identity, role and area of responsibility.

Minds can help with email, participate in Projects, complete Tasks, collaborate on the same objective and handle useful work on a schedule. You set the goals, provide the context and approve the results. Your Minds help move the work forward.

## How is Minds Studio different from a normal AI chat?

A normal AI conversation often ends when you close the chat. The next time you return, you may need to explain the background again, find the right files and remember what still needs to happen.

Minds Studio is designed for work that continues. It brings together:

- Email and connected accounts
- AI collaborators with different roles
- Voice, dictation and chat
- Projects with instructions and files
- A Taskboard for following progress
- Multi-Mind collaboration
- Scheduled agent work
- A Dashboard with activity and recaps
- Discover, World mode and Pods

Think of it as moving from **“ask AI a question”** to **“give an AI team a goal and follow the work.”**

## Manage your email—or delegate the work to a Mind

Email is an important part of **Work mode**.

The Email tab provides a familiar place to manage and send messages manually. You remain in control of your communication and can write an email yourself whenever you want.

When a message requires more than a quick response, you can delegate the work to a Mind. A Mind could help you:

- Summarize a long email conversation
- Identify questions that still need answers
- Extract dates, requests and responsibilities
- Prepare a response for your review
- Compare the email with a Project file
- Suggest the next steps
- Turn follow-up actions into Tasks

Imagine receiving a detailed client request. You could handle it manually, or ask a Mind:

> Summarize what the client needs, identify anything that is unclear and prepare the next steps.

If the email relates to a Project, its instructions and files can provide additional context.

**Email arrives → delegate to a Mind → review the result → reply or continue as a Task.**

Minds do not each automatically receive their own email address. Mail is part of the Work experience, where you decide what to manage manually and what to delegate.

*Work mode brings Mail and work together, while connected accounts keep different identities available in one experience.*

## Meet your Minds

A **Mind** is an AI collaborator with a purpose. You might work with:

- A Research Mind that gathers and explains information
- A Content Mind that develops articles and social posts
- A Project Mind that organizes plans and next steps
- A Strategy Mind that challenges ideas
- A Daily Briefing Mind that summarizes useful updates

Each Mind can have a recognizable identity, role, character and voice. You can see whether it is working, quiet or waiting for you.

![A team of Minds](assets/screenshots/01-minds-chat.jpg)

*Different Minds can support different parts of your work.*

## Talk, type or use dictation

Minds Studio supports chat, dictation and voice. Type when you want to be precise, dictate a quick idea or talk through a complicated problem.

A conversation can begin casually and become organized work when it needs more structure.

![Voice and dictation controls](assets/screenshots/13-chat-voice.jpg)

## Give important work a Project

A **Project** is the shared home for a goal or ongoing piece of work. It can contain:

- The Project description
- Private instructions and background context
- Reference files
- Conversations
- Related Tasks

![Project context](assets/screenshots/08-project-context.jpg)

*Projects keep instructions, files, conversations and Tasks connected to the same goal.*

When you ask a Mind for help inside a Project, you do not have to repeat the entire background every time. Projects are useful for campaigns, research, clients, events, content series and other work that continues beyond one conversation.

## Turn conversations into trackable Tasks

Important work should not disappear inside an email thread or chat history. The **Taskboard** turns requests into visible work.

![Minds Studio Taskboard](assets/screenshots/06-task-board.jpg)

Tasks can move through clear stages:

- **To do** — ready to begin
- **Doing** — currently being worked on
- **Needs you** — waiting for a decision or more information
- **Check it** — ready for review
- **Done** — reviewed and completed

Minds can move the work forward, while you retain control of important decisions and final approval.

## Ask several Minds to work on one objective

With a **Multi-Mind Task**, you can assign the same objective to several Minds. Each Mind receives a separate assignment and develops its own approach.

For a campaign, a Research Mind might study the audience, a Content Mind might propose creative directions and a Strategy Mind might challenge the positioning.

![Assigning a task to multiple Minds](assets/screenshots/07-multi-mind-task.jpg)

*Each Mind receives its own Task card, making progress and results easy to compare.*

## See the bigger picture on your Dashboard

The **Dashboard** helps you understand what is happening across the workspace.

![Minds Studio Dashboard](assets/screenshots/05-dashboard.jpg)

You can see which Minds are working, quiet or waiting for you, along with open Tasks, estimated time saved, energy and activity. Daily and weekly **recaps** summarize what moved forward and where your attention is needed.

## Discover ideas and turn them into action

**Discover** is a place to browse ideas, stories and useful work shared through the wider Minds community.

![Minds Studio Discover](assets/screenshots/10-discover.jpg)

Choose **Ask a Mind** to explore an item in a private conversation. Ask the Mind to explain it, find another perspective, connect it to a Project or turn it into an actionable idea.

If you want similar information regularly, choose **Get this daily**. Preview the Task, choose a Mind and review the schedule before activating anything.

**Discover something → ask a Mind → turn it into a useful routine.**

## Let recurring work happen on schedule

Scheduled work can support activities that are valuable because they happen consistently: a morning briefing, daily trend scan, weekly Project summary or recurring progress check.

You decide what should happen, which Mind is responsible, when it should run and where the result belongs. Scheduled work remains visible through Tasks, the Dashboard and recaps.

## Move between Work mode and World mode

**Work mode** brings together Mail, Projects, Tasks and the Minds helping you complete work.

**World mode** offers a broader, more social experience for discovering ideas and interacting beyond your immediate Projects.

The modes help separate focused work from wider exploration.

## Bring several Minds together with Pods

A **Pod** brings several Minds together so they can work as a team.

For example, a launch Pod might combine a Research Mind, Strategy Mind, Content Mind and Project Mind around the same objective.

Pods are currently experimental. To access them:

1. Switch Minds Studio to **Work mode**.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Expand **Experimental**.
4. Turn on **Pods**.
5. Open Pods from the main sidebar or **More**.

![Pods in Experimental settings](assets/screenshots/04-pods-agent-computer.jpg)

The Pods setting applies only to the current device, and the feature may change while it remains experimental.

## What could your first day look like?

Start with one real workflow:

1. Connect the account you want to use.
2. Open an email that requires more than a quick reply.
3. Delegate the email-related work to a Mind.
4. Ask the Mind to summarize the request and prepare next steps.
5. Connect the work to a Project.
6. Add useful instructions or reference files.
7. Turn follow-up actions into Tasks.
8. Ask a second Mind for another perspective.
9. Review the results on the Taskboard.
10. Check the Dashboard for the overall activity.
11. Send the final email when you are satisfied.
12. Schedule a recurring version only if it would be genuinely helpful.

By the end, you have not simply generated an AI response. You have moved real work from a message to an organized and reviewable result.

Minds Studio connects email, AI collaboration, Projects, Tasks and recurring work.

Email brings in conversations and requests. Minds help understand and develop the work. Projects provide context. Tasks make progress visible. Multi-Mind collaboration provides different perspectives. The Dashboard and recaps help you remain in control.

You provide the goals, judgment and boundaries. Your Minds help move the work forward.

**Start with one Mind, one Project and one real task. Then see what your AI team can do.**
