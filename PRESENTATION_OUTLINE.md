# Presentation outline — Introducing Minds Studio

1. **Minds Studio** — AI teammates with identity, context and accountability.
2. **From chat to delegation** — assign real work by chat, email or voice.
3. **Identity and access** — passwordless entry, multiple accounts, send-as boundaries.
4. **A Mind is persistent** — profile, voice, skills, apps, memory and energy.
5. **Projects hold context** — private instructions, conversations and files.
6. **Tasks make work visible** — To do → Doing → Needs you → Check it → Done.
7. **Teams of Minds** — parallel specialist assignments with per-agent accountability.
8. **Operational visibility** — dashboard, recaps, cost, energy and time saved.
9. **From discovery to routine** — Discover, Work/World modes and recurring services.
10. **The next layer** — Pods, Minds Computer, scheduled work and responsible autonomy.
