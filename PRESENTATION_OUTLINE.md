# Presentation outline — Introducing Minds Studio

1. **Minds Studio** — AI collaborators with identity, context and visible accountability.
2. **Mail becomes work** — in Work mode, send email manually or delegate the work inside a message to a Mind.
3. **Identity and access** — passwordless entry, multiple connected accounts and clear sending boundaries.
4. **A Mind has a role** — profile, voice, skills, apps, memory and energy.
5. **Projects hold context** — private instructions, conversations and files.
6. **Tasks make work visible** — To do → Doing → Needs you → Check it → Done.
7. **Multi-Mind Tasks** — one objective, separate specialist assignments and progress cards.
8. **Operational visibility** — Dashboard, recaps, energy, spend and estimated time saved.
9. **From discovery to routine** — Ask a Mind, Get this daily and scheduled work.
10. **Different spaces** — focused Work mode and broader World mode.
11. **Pods** — experimental teams of Minds, enabled through Settings → Experimental → Pods in Work mode.
12. **Responsible autonomy** — human review, clear boundaries, stoppable work and transparent activity.

Use the screenshot gallery as visual evidence. Do not state that every Mind automatically has an email address; Mail is a Work-mode surface that can become a source of delegated work.
